## 2024-05-18 - Avoid iterrows() in Pandas for large DataFrames
**Learning:** The `iterrows()` method in Pandas operates with O(N) complexity for loops but effectively creates an O(N*M) bottleneck when filtering large DataFrames inside the loop, significantly degrading performance. In `automated_report2.py`, this anti-pattern was used to filter and sum DataFrames sequentially.
**Action:** Replace `iterrows()` with fully vectorized operations wherever possible. For aggregations and joins across different DataFrames based on a key, use `groupby(key)[col].sum()` to pre-calculate sums, followed by `.map()` to map the aggregated values. This converts O(N*M) complexity down to O(N+M) and is substantially faster. Also use `np.where()` for vectorized conditional logic rather than row-by-row iteration.
